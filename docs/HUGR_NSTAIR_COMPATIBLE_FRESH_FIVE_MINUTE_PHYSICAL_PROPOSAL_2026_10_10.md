1. Wear the Watch normally. Keep Honor nearby, on its existing normal companion connection; do not migrate to the Fold/Samsung in this gate. Any pairing/reset/setup warning is STOP, not a troubleshooting invitation.
2. Open normal Main HUGR on the compatible Watch once. Press **Start fresh 5 minutes** once. Do not press retained-v2 delivery, receipt-only, keep-awake or other controls. Passive launcher opening itself does not record. Stop on admission/start failure or a permission choice not covered by the final admitted procedure. Once permission handling is complete, note the fresh **STARTED** source/run identity and time once. STARTED is admission evidence, not proof of radio readiness or valid sensor data.
3. Promptly open normal Phone61 HUGR on Honor and press **Connect Galaxy Watch** once. Keep that Phone screen foreground during this bounded first rehearsal. No relaunch/reconnect/retry; no credential/haptic/shadow/observer action. There is no routine Watch filming or scrolling requirement.
4. Let recording stop/finalize automatically at five minutes. **Do not press a manual stop at five minutes.** Observe the Phone's **SAVED RUN** panel until the first terminal result/failure or **20 minutes from fresh Watch admission**, whichever comes first. Stop on crash/black screen, disconnect, failed source/receipt/custody, unexpected prompt or state, or deadline. A segment ACK, live beats, STARTED, caught-up index or successful Phone write is not whole-run acceptance.
5. Acceptance requires **SAVED RUN: COMPLETE_VERIFIED**, durable exact Watch-origin COMPLETED receipt for the same actual source, full final canonical manifest inventory/count/ranges/bytes/hashes, all exact Watch-accepted ACK tuples, required canonical streams recorded, and no acceptance gaps. Record the Phone source UUID and receipt revision once. FAILED/PARTIAL remains failed/partial; do not reconnect to improve it.
6. **Only after the separately authorized terminal-success restart/readback step:** use Honor **Settings → Apps → HUGR → Force stop** (not Clear storage/cache and not uninstall). This ends Main and its in-process state without deleting data. Then open only **HUGR Saved Source Evidence**, the separate information “i” launcher, once. This starts the passive app process; do not reopen Main or press Connect. Stop at the first report/failure or 60 seconds without report; no retry.
7. The offline report must retain the same source/Watch receipt identity and show a successful **new canonical custody recheck**, matching full bytes/manifests/ranges/hashes, **watch_receipt_proof_current_recheck=MATCH** and **instrumentResult=COMPLETE_VERIFIED**. Routine operator burden: one Watch launcher/start action, one Phone Connect, one saved result, one post-process-stop offline report. Watch diagnostics only if a later failure authority explicitly includes them. Lost shutdown confirmation does not erase a previously durable exact result; receipt-only recovery is a separate bounded decision, not an automatic retry.
