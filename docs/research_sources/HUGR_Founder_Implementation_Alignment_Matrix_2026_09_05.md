# HUGR Founder–Implementation Alignment

**Bounded RUN# audit · 5 September 2026 · Manus AI**

> **Purpose:** establish where founder intent, documented design, executable reality and current evidence align, differ or remain unknown. This is not a new Bible, implementation authority, interface redesign or scientific validation.

## Executive disposition

Moustache’s mature reconstruction is **substantially aligned with HUGR’s canonical direction**, particularly on human authority, uncertainty preservation, prospective learning and the distinction between an opinionated HUGR application and a possible reusable substrate. The important corrections concern **tense and evidence state**: the reconstruction often describes what HUGR is *trying to learn* as though the active system has already learned it. Current evidence supports a serious measurement/evidence substrate, a source-wired prospective shadow loop and several device-tested engineering paths; it does **not** support validated personalized physiological intelligence, prediction before awareness, haptic efficacy or platform generality.[1] [3] [5] [6]

The archaeology’s runtime-fork conclusion also survives, with one refinement. Build 26’s Samsung/`WatchConnect` redirection was at least partly an explicit contemporary scope choice, not a wholly unexplained disappearance. The evidence still does not reveal why the advanced engine was not integrated, whether that omission was recognized as a North-Star break, or who understood its consequence. Those questions remain **UNKNOWN**, not retrospectively rationalized.[1] [9] [10]

## Exact empirical discriminator ladder

| Question | Cheapest sufficient discriminator | Claim ceiling if passed |
|---|---|---|
| **Can HUGR preserve an ordinary waking day?** | Complete the frozen foreground entrance, one 8–12-hour no-repair ordinary-life rehearsal, and one phone→protected-ledger→export→reverse-trace window with explicit gaps/quality.[12] | Measurement/data-integrity feasibility on the named pair. |
| **Do current estimates relate usefully to reported experience?** | Freeze estimator and quality rules; prospectively compare quality-eligible estimates with contemporaneous reports, class prevalence and uncertainty. | Concordance/calibration description; not physiological truth. |
| **Does personalization improve prediction?** | Walk-forward frozen predictions against persistence, frequency and frozen heuristic comparators; score before eligible update; retain abstentions and contamination.[3] [6] | Prospective improvement for the tested target/population only. |
| **Does HUGR notice before awareness?** | Silent prospective transition target plus explicit first-awareness event distinct from report time and fixed lead-time scoring.[8] [12] | Lead-time evidence; not consciousness or causality. |
| **Is asking useful?** | Predeclared query/no-query opportunities, policy version, ethical allocation/withholding, information-gain score and burden outcome. | Utility of that bounded query policy. |
| **Do haptics help?** | Device-verified delivery/perception, eligible opportunities, assigned/withheld action, carryover control and predefined proximal outcomes.[5] [12] | Haptic feasibility/effect under the tested design; not therapy. |
| **Is a reusable Labs substrate real?** | Implement a second honest platform adapter without changing canonical identity/quality contracts, then run an empirical comparability protocol. | Technical portability and measured comparability; not universal physiology. |

## Shared map reached

**Neil intends** a humane system that learns enough about one person’s changing physiology and context to sometimes support earlier self-recognition with minimal burden. **Manus actually built** substantial sensing, transport, evidence, interface and inference machinery, but the original advanced engine and active Samsung runtime diverged; later work recovered a safer shadow prospective loop and a much stronger evidence substrate rather than blindly reconnecting the old stack. **Current evidence supports** engineering components, bounded device behaviours, protected memory/audit infrastructure and deterministically tested prospective-loop mechanics. **It does not support** validated personalized physiology, prediction before awareness, haptic efficacy, clinical utility or a general HUGR Labs platform. Those remain hypotheses or require the explicit empirical discriminators above.

> **Bottom line for Moustache:** your conceptual maturation is mostly compatible with the canonical direction. The central correction is grammatical but consequential: replace **“HUGR learns useful longitudinal structure”** with **“HUGR is being built to test whether qualified longitudinal evidence can support useful individual estimates, questions and actions.”** Reality retains veto power.
