He asks whether that original angle has been lost and whether an eight-hour run would still depend on scrolling and filming the Watch.

These are **preserved implementation/design**, not a new confirmation of live deployment, credentials, eight-hour operation, end-to-end clock alignment or a working autonomous Manus observer. No portal/database/device session was opened or tested in this clarification pass.

## Why the focus became narrower

An observer cannot reliably explain a run if retained source identity, sample custody, finalization, exact delivery and timing cannot be trusted. The recent work repaired named lower-layer failures and exposed the difference between app labels and actual canonical evidence. This is necessary infrastructure for the original idea, not a replacement for it.

But it also revealed a genuine practical gap: Neil was being used as the cross-device log collector. Scrolling and filming are fallback evidence methods, not a product operating model. We should not call that eight-hour readiness merely because acquisition can survive eight hours.

Watch77 durable local diagnostics reduced dependence on wireless ADB. Phone60's admitted offline evidence reader is the next local custody readback tool. Neither is an automatic Watch→Phone→portal operational reporting path, and Phone60 does not give Manus live access to data or durable complete ACK history.

## Recommended minimum before an eight-hour physical gate

Keep this a small operational completion slice within the existing architecture, not a fresh telemetry platform:

1. **A durable per-run summary on the Phone:** actual session identity; source duration/timing uncertainty; available streams and counts with honest missingness; recorded/queued/received/verified stages; exact finalization and delivery evidence; stop/failure reason. A current live indicator is not a stored-data count.
2. **A reviewed, consented evidence handoff:** authorized selected evidence to the existing protected portal, preserving identity and clocks, with buffering/retry/idempotency and explicit unknown/stale status when offline. No automatic private-data transfer is authorized by this recommendation.
3. **An evidence-based Watchtower view:** separate acquisition, BLE liveness, Phone custody and portal receipt. It must not say recording failed just because a companion connection is grey, or say data is complete merely because a device is Connected.
4. **A bounded observer role:** Manus can inspect authorized evidence and explain what happened or propose a focused next check. Start with a reviewed on-demand report as the smallest possible mode; continuous or periodic observation remains a separate decision. Ordinary hardware logging must work even when Manus is absent.
5. **A shorter rehearsal of the actual operating workflow:** screen sleeping, interruption, stop/finalization and report readback without routine Watch filming; assess storage/battery/quality/timing and operator burden before increasing duration.

This is an engineering recommendation, not a claim that those capabilities are implemented or validated. Screenshots/video may still be useful for an exceptional UI defect or a haptic-perception study; they should not be needed to reconstruct every ordinary run.

## Where Manus belongs

The smallest credible meaning of “inside the project during N1” is an authorized observer of the evidence layer, not a general-purpose LLM running on the Watch. Watch handles bounded acquisition, tactile interaction and durable recording; Phone handles custody and a usable run summary; portal handles protected history/analysis; Manus reads permitted evidence and supports engineering/research decisions. Supportive closed-loop haptics and adaptive foreground policy remain separately governed Book One questions, not implied by an observer.
